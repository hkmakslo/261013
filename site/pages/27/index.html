Quick workaround:Cantonese with HeyGen.

To create a video in Cantonese:

Watch on YouTube ↗
  1. 01Download the Notebook video.
  2. 02Translate it into Cantonese in HeyGen.
  3. 03Listen, check and share the reviewed version.
Open HeyGen Translate ↗

HeyGen lists Chinese (Cantonese - Traditional) among its supported translation languages. HeyGen’s supported video-translation languages ↗ · HeyGen video-translation workflow guidance ↗

Practical tip

For slide-based videos, audio-only translation may be enough; lip sync is only relevant to visible speakers.

HeyGen is a separate service, not Notebook Studio.

Use only school-approved, non-sensitive content.

Review meaning and pronunciation before sharing.
