Workshop/Ten prompts/01

Check materials against HKDSE requirements

Situation

Before planning S6 revision, an English teacher wants to know whether the school’s reading-comprehension notes address the skills identified in the selected official guidance.

Sources to add to Notebook

the applicable English Language Curriculum and Assessment Guide; the applicable English Language assessment framework; your teacher-authored notes, labelled “S6 Reading Revision — teacher notes”. The notes should contain the actual explanations and practice you want checked, not just a contents page.

EDB English curriculum documents ↗HKEAA assessment frameworks ↗

Use the official documents applicable to your cohort. Add your own authorised teaching notes; they are not supplied by this website.

Use one language version at a time. These are worked examples: adapt the source names to match your notebook.

English promptMarkdown / plain-text prompt

Compare ‘S6 Reading Revision — teacher notes’ with the selected HKDSE English Language Curriculum and Assessment Guide and assessment framework. Focus on reading comprehension and supporting answers with text evidence.

Use a four-column table: Official objective; Evidence in our notes; Coverage (clear / partial / not found); Suggested improvement. Cite both the guidance and our notes. End with three revision-teaching priorities.

Keep curriculum objectives separate from exam rules. Flag anything not stated in the sources. ‘Not found’ refers only to the selected notes.

Write in English.

繁體中文提示詞Markdown / plain-text prompt

請把「S6 Reading Revision — teacher notes」與所選HKDSE英文科《課程及評估指引》及評核大綱比較，集中於閱讀理解及以文本證據支持答案。

用四欄表格：官方目標；教材證據；涵蓋程度（清楚／部分／未找到）；改進建議。引用官方資料及筆記，最後列出三項溫習教學重點。

區分課程目標與考試規則。資料未有說明的內容，請指出。「未找到」只指所選筆記。

請以繁體中文回答。
Adapt this task: show the reusable templates

English promptMarkdown / plain-text prompt

I teach HKDSE [subject] to [level]. Compare our [topic] notes with the selected Curriculum and Assessment Guide and assessment framework.

Use a four-column table: Official objective; Evidence in our notes; Coverage (clear / partial / not found); Suggested improvement. Cite both the guidance and our notes. End with three teaching priorities.

Keep curriculum objectives separate from exam rules. Flag anything not stated in the sources. ‘Not found’ refers only to the selected notes.

Write in English.

繁體中文提示詞Markdown / plain-text prompt

我教授HKDSE[科目]，學生為[年級]。請把[課題]筆記與所選《課程及評估指引》及評核大綱比較。

用四欄表格：官方目標；教材證據；涵蓋程度（清楚／部分／未找到）；改進建議。引用官方資料及筆記，最後列出三項備課重點。

區分課程目標與考試規則。資料未有說明的內容，請指出。「未找到」只指所選筆記。

請以繁體中文回答。
